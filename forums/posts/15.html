UTILITIES:

  • 9-INCH GLASS PIE PLATE
  • ALUMINUM FOIL
  • SPATULA
INGREDIENTS:
  • PIE CRUST MIX
  • 1/2 CUP MILK
  • CAN OF CREAM OF CHICKEN SOUP
  • ROTISSERIE CHICKEN PEELED ON THE BREAST SIDE
  • 2 CUPS FROZEN BAG OF MIXED VEGETABLES (PEAS AND CARROTS)
  • 1/3 BUTTER
  • 1/2 YELLOW ONION (WORKS BEST, WHITE WORKS TOO)
  • 2 CLOVES OF GARLIC
  • THYME
  • OREGANO
  • OLIVE OIL
  • SALT
  • PEPPER
  • AN EGG
PREPARATION (SEPARATE OFF TO THE SIDE):
  1. PEEL SKINLESS ROTISSERIE CHICKEN’S BREAST APART AND TEAR OFF MEDIUM SIZED PIECES
  2. CUT YELLOW ONION IN HALF, ALONGSIDE THE STEMS ENDS. REMOVE OUTER LAYER FOR HIGHER QUALITY. DICE THE PREFERRED HALF!
  3. CUT THE ENDS OF TWO GARLIC CLOVES, REMOVE SKIN AND PUT INTO GARLIC PRESS.
  4. MICROWAVE FOR 2 MINUTES A PACK OF PREFERRED MIXED VEGETABLES (PEAS AND CARROTS WORK BEST) TO DEFROST.
  5. TAKE ONE ROLL OF PIE CRUST, AND COVER THE 9 INCH GLASS PIE PLATE UNTIL ALL SIDES AND BOTTOM ARE FULLY COVERED BY THE PIE CRUST ROLL.
COOKING
  1. POUR OLIVE OIL INTO SAUTÉ PAN, POUR DICED HALF ONION IN ON MEDIUM TO LOWER HEAT, COOK AND STIR UNTIL ONION BITS TURN CLEAR
  2. ADD IN 2 PRESSED GARLIC CLOVES, LET IT SIT FOR 10 SECONDS OR TILL YOU SMELL IT! POUR IN CHICKEN RIGHT AS SOON AS YOU DO, FOR A DECENT AMOUNT OF TIME TO COOK BETTER!
  3. THROW IN BUTTER TO SWEETEN CHICKEN’S TASTE, MIXED TILL BUTTER FULLY MELTED.
  4. ADD IN ENTIRE CAN OF CREAM OF CHICKEN SOUP FOR THE CONSISTENCY! FOLLOWED BY THE 1/2 CUP OF MILK.
  5. KEEP STIRRING TILL EVERYTHING IS FULLY MIXED, CREAM OF CHICKEN AND MILK BECOME SAME COLOR
  6. START PREHEATING OVEN TO 425°.
  7. KEEP STIRRING AND AFTER A MINUTE, ADD IN THE MICROWAVED BAG OF FROZEN VEGETABLES AND SPICES! (SPICES SHOULD BE POURED IN AS FEELS RIGHT, AND COVERS THE SURFACE OF THE SAUCE. I DONT KNOW!)
  8. MIX TILL BUBBLES FORM AND POP, FEELS RIGHT, OR BECOMES SLIGHTLY THICKER. THEN POUR THE MIXTURE INTO THE PREPARED GLASS PIE PLATE!
  9. COVER THE MIXTURE IN THE PLATE WITH YET ANOTHER PIE CRUST. CUT TWO OR MORE THIN SLITS WITH A SHARP KNIFE INTO THE SURFACE TO LET IT VENT OUT WHEN LATER IN THE OVEN.
  10. USE A BASTING BRUSH TO COVER THE SURFACE WITH BEATEN EGG. ONLY ENOUGH TO COVER THE SURFACE!! GO MAKE AN OMELETTE OR SOMETHING WITH THE REST
  11. COVER THE PIE WITH ALUMINUM FOIL, AND PUT INTO THE PREPARED, ALREADY PREHEATED OVEN FOR 15 MINUTES TO COOK INSIDE BETTER. THEN, TAKE OUT OF THE OVEN, REMOVE THE ALUMINUM FOIL, AND PUT IT BACK IN FOR 14 MINUTES, OR UNTIL GOLDEN BROWN, OR CRUSTED!
  12. LET IT SIT FOR A FEW MINUTES, AND ENJOY THE PIE DAMMIT!
